# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Faro exists to help people in their worst moments. We hold contributors to the
same standard: treat everyone with dignity and good faith. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the people Faro serves

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.
